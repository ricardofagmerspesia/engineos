## What
Wave 4(b) of the TypeScript runtime migration (plan Tasks 30-32): `scripts/run-evals.sh`, `scripts/bench-runtime.sh` and `scripts/usage-report.sh` become shims over `engineos evals`, `engineos bench` and `engineos usage-report`, implemented in `src/evals/`, `src/bench/` and `src/report/`. The eval runner's `deferrals_of` closure derivation moves to a shared `src/lib/deferrals.ts`; the bench loses its embedded python3; the usage report loses jq.

## Why
Plan Wave 4. These are the last runtime paths outside the validator that need jq or python3; CI's "Evals (deterministic tier)" and "Runtime performance budgets" steps keep calling the `.sh` paths.

## Scope
`src/evals/{run,deterministic,live}.ts` + tests, `src/lib/deferrals.ts` + test, `src/bench/*.ts` + tests, `src/report/usage.ts` + test, `src/cli.ts` (three arms), the three `scripts/*.sh` shims, `docs/COMPATIBILITY.md` (1.7.0 section), `dist/engineos.cjs`.
