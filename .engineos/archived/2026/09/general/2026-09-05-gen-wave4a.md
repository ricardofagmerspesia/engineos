## What
Wave 4(a) of the TypeScript runtime migration (plan Tasks 28-29): `scripts/gen-agent-commands.sh` and `scripts/gen-agent-registry.sh` become shims over `engineos gen agent-commands` / `engineos gen agent-registry`, implemented in `src/gen/` with a shared frontmatter reader. Output must be byte-identical: CI's two drift guards (`git diff --quiet -- commands/agents` and `-- registries/agents.json` after a run) are the acceptance.

## Why
Plan Wave 4. The two generators are the last `jq` consumers outside the validator; porting them removes `jq` from the generator path and puts the frontmatter parsing (which the validator deliberately re-implements independently) in one tested module.

## Scope
`src/gen/{frontmatter,agent-commands,agent-registry,cli}.ts` + tests, `src/cli.ts` (`gen` arm), `scripts/gen-agent-commands.sh` and `scripts/gen-agent-registry.sh` (shims), `scripts/checks/10-rules.sh` (C1 prose), `README.md`, `docs/ADOPTION.md`, `docs/COMPATIBILITY.md` (1.7.0 section), `dist/engineos.cjs`.

- Six `commands/agents/*.md` carry no marker (analyst, android-builder, architecture-analyst, elias, ios-builder, mobile-coordinator); every run reports them preserved, as before.
- `registries/agents.json` description still names `scripts/gen-agent-registry.sh` on purpose: it is committed output and changing it would trip the drift guard on every consumer.
